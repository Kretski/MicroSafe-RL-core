## 2. Commercial agreement (optional)

A separate paid agreement is available for organisations that need something
Apache-2.0 explicitly does not provide. Apache-2.0 disclaims all warranties and
all liability; a commercial agreement can add:

- **Warranty and liability terms**, which the open license disclaims entirely
- **Indemnification**, where a customer's procurement or legal process requires it
- **Certification support** — documentation packages, traceability evidence, and
  engineering time toward IEC 61508 / ISO 26262 / DO-178C qualification
- **Validation on your plant** — parameter tuning, closed-loop analysis, and
  hardware-in-the-loop testing for your specific system
- **Prioritised support and defined response times**
- **Relicensing** for use in a codebase where attribution or notice obligations
  are impractical

The value being sold is assurance and engineering effort, not access to the
source. The source is free.

The Apache-2.0 grant is irrevocable for the versions released under it. Adopting
the commercial agreement is never required to use, evaluate, or ship the
software.

The author is the sole copyright holder and retains the right to license the
work under other terms. Contributions accepted into the repository are taken
under Apache-2.0; if this changes, a contributor licensing agreement will be
introduced and stated explicitly rather than applied retroactively.

## Current status

MicroSafe-RL Core is experimental. It is not certified for safety-critical
deployment, and its benefit relative to plain output clipping has not yet been
demonstrated by benchmark. Commercial engagements at this stage are best framed
as joint evaluation rather than as procurement of a qualified component.
